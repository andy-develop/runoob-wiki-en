C# 运算符

An operator is a symbol that tells the compiler to perform specific mathematical or logical operations. C# has rich built-in operators, categorized as follows:

  • 算术运算符
  • 关系运算符
  • 逻辑运算符
  • 位运算符
  • 赋值运算符
  • 其他运算符

This tutorial will explain arithmetic operators, relational operators, logical operators, bitwise operators, assignment operators, and other operators one by one.

运算符描述实例
+Adds two operands.A + B will result in 30
-Subtracts the second operand from the first operand.A - B will result in -10
*Multiplies two operands.A * B will result in 200
/Numerator divided by denominatorB / A will result in 2
%Modulus operator, remainder after integer division.B % A will result in 0
++Increment operator, increases an integer value by 1.A++ will result in 11

位运算符

Bitwise operators act on bits and perform operations bit by bit. The truth tables for &, |, and ^ are as follows:

pqp & qp | qp ^ q
00000
01011
11110
10011

Suppose A = 60 and B = 13. Now expressed in binary format, they are as follows:

A = 0011 1100

B = 0000 1101

-----------------

A&B = 0000 1100

A|B = 0011 1101

A^B = 0011 0001

~A  = 1100 0011

The following table lists the bitwise operators supported by C#. Assume variableAhas a value of 60, variableBhas a value of 13, then:

运算符描述实例
&If present in both operands, the binary AND operator copies a bit to the result.(A & B) 将得到 12，即为 0000 1100
|If present in either operand, the binary OR operator copies a bit to the result.(A | B) 将得到 61，即为 0011 1101
^If present in one operand but not both, the binary XOR operator copies a bit to the result.(A ^ B) 将得到 49，即为 0011 0001
~The bitwise complement operator is a unary operator that has the effect of "flipping" bits, that is, 0 becomes 1 and 1 becomes 0, including the sign bit.(~A ) will result in -61, which is 1100 0011, the two's complement form of a signed binary number.
<<Binary left shift operator. The value of the left operand is shifted left by the number of bits specified by the right operand.A << 2 将得到 240，即为 1111 0000
>>Binary right shift operator. The value of the left operand is shifted right by the number of bits specified by the right operand.A >> 2 将得到 15，即为 0000 1111

Operator precedence in C#

Operator precedence determines the grouping of terms in an expression. This affects how an expression is evaluated. Some operators have higher precedence than others. For example, multiplication and division operators have higher precedence than addition and subtraction operators.

For example x = 7 + 3 * 2, here x is assigned 13, not 20, because the operator * has higher precedence than +, so the multiplication 3*2 is calculated first, and then 7 is added.

The following table lists operators by operator precedence from high to low. Operators with higher precedence appear at the top of the table, and operators with lower precedence appear at the bottom. In an expression, operators with higher precedence are evaluated first.

A simple overview of precedence.: Parentheses first, then multiplication/division, then addition/subtraction, then shifts, then relational, then logical, then conditional, and finally comma., 。

    &，|，^ In addition to bitwise operations, they can also be used for logical operations, corresponding to AND, OR, and XOR respectively.

    ^ 运算符The binary ^ operator is predefined for integral types and bool. For integral types, ^ computes the bitwise XOR of its operands. For bool operands, ^ computes the logical XOR of its operands; that is, the result is true if and only if exactly one of its operands is true.

    Console.WriteLine(true ^ false);  // 返回 true
Console.WriteLine(false ^ false);  // 返回 false
Console.WriteLine(true ^ true);  // 返回 false

    | 运算符The binary | operator is predefined for integral types and bool. For integral types, | computes the bitwise OR of its operands. For bool operands, | computes the logical OR of its operands; that is, the result is false if and only if both of its operands are false.

    Console.WriteLine(true | false);  // 返回 true
Console.WriteLine(false | false); // 返回 false

    & 运算符The binary & operator is predefined for integral types and bool. For integral types, & computes the logical bitwise AND of its operands. For bool operands, & computes the logical AND of its operands; that is, the result is true if and only if both of its operands are true.

    Console.WriteLine(true & false);  // 返回 false
Console.WriteLine(true & true);   // 返回 true

    Among them, the operation results of & and | are exactly the same as && and ||, but && and || have better performance, because both && and || check the value of the first operand, and if the result can already be determined, they do not process the second operand at all.

    比如：

    bool a = true;               
bool b = false;              
bool c = a || b;

    When evaluating the first operand a, if c is already true, there is no need to process the second operand b.

    C# 是用<<(左移) 和 >>（右移）The operator is used to perform shift operations.

    Left shift (<<): Shifts the first operand left by the number of bits specified by the second operand, and the vacated positions are filled with 0.

    Left shift is equivalent to multiplication. Shifting left by one bit is equivalent to multiplying by 2; shifting left by two bits is equivalent to multiplying by 4; shifting left by three bits is equivalent to multiplying by 8.

    x<<1= x*2
x<<2= x*4
x<<3= x*8
x<<4= x*16

    Similarly, right shift is the opposite:

    Right shift (>>): Shifts the first operand right by the number of bits specified by the second operand, and the vacated positions are filled with 0.

    Right shift is equivalent to integer division. Shifting right by one bit is equivalent to dividing by 2; shifting right by two bits is equivalent to dividing by 4; shifting right by three bits is equivalent to dividing by 8.

    x>>1= x/2
x>>2= x/4
x>>3= x/8
x>>4= x/16

    Because bit shifting is faster than multiplication and division, for operations with high efficiency requirements, and when multiplication and division by powers of 2 are satisfied, bit shifting can be used.
