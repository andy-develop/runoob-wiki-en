Twelve Tips to Make Your Computer Desk More Comfortable

Category Technology News

1. Raise your computer screen to eye level.

This is very good for your cervical spine. Buy a computer stand, or make one yourself, or simply stack a few books to raise it up—that works well too.

2. Use a beautiful, high-quality desk mat.

3. Use a very stylish pen holder

Not only does it add greenery, but it also adds charm

4. Use a branch-shaped pendant lamp

5. Get a mouse pad with a pattern

6. Use a compact, multi-functional little box to store various daily necessities.

7. Change your chair to a hammock-style seat

It's best to ask your boss first whether this is allowed.

8. Buy yourself a nice throw pillow

9. Buy yourself a nice wool seat cushion.

10. Prepare a footrest that allows you to move your feet.

Prevent legs from cramping or going numb after sitting for long periods

11. Use a suitable desk lamp

12. Switch to a keyboard with wooden keys
