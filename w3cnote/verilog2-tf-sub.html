8.3 Verilog TF Subroutine List

Categories Verilog Tutorial: Advanced

To preserve the authenticity of the subroutines and avoid a poor translation, all subroutine lists retain their English descriptions. It is recommended to carefully study the corresponding types of subroutines before using them. Not all subroutines have been verified here; due to version or transcription issues, there may be omissions. Corrections and comments are welcome.

TF subroutines are often used for bidirectional data transfer between Verilog and user C subroutines. When a subroutine prefixed with tf_ has a corresponding subroutine prefixed with tf_i, the instance pointer on which operations occur in the tf_i-prefixed corresponding subroutine must be passed as an additional variable at the end of the variable list.

TF subroutines have no explicit classification markers in their naming conventions. The following is a simple summary.
