Categories Programming Technology

Given that many users have reported the need for a user center to record their personal learning progress, Runoob Tutorial is officially opening its user center to the public. However, since the features are still being improved, and to prevent abusive registration, invitation-based registration is required.

1. Login/Registration Interface

Login and registration do not have separate pages; they are both pop-up dialogs. You can click the login button in the navigation bar to open the pop-up.

2. User Center Page

The user center currently has relatively few features. Many users have reported the need for personal learning records; currently, only 10 records are provided, and any excess will be automatically replaced.
