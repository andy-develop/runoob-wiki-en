2014 Economically Applicable Man/Woman Standards Released: How Many Do You Meet?

Categories Miscellaneous

Recently, the standard for the "economically applicable man" has been widely circulated online. This standard covers many aspects, such as physical condition, occupation, and personality. Economically applicable men who meet or partially meet these standards are favored by female netizens and are considered to have the most potential to be good husbands and good fathers.

2014 Economically Applicable Man Standards:Height 172-182cm, weight 65-85kg, ordinary hairstyle, gentle temperament, no smoking, little drinking, not fond of bar-hopping, bachelor's degree or above, monthly salary between 3000-10000 yuan, able to cook, patient, filial, caring, motivated, refined in manners, no cursing, modest, cautious, steady, generous, loyal and unwavering in love, responsible.

How many do you meet?

Of course, if there are economically applicable men, there are correspondingly economically applicable women. An economically applicable woman is a single woman of appropriate age who is devoted and diligent, may not have a high salary, but has a stable income.

Economically Applicable Woman Standards:Height 161-169, weight 43-58kg, bust B-C cup, long hair down to shoulders, gentle temperament, not materialistic, not boy-crazy, a little secretly sultry, college degree or above (including full-time, self-study, adult education, etc.), monthly salary between 3000-6000 yuan, able to do laundry, able to cook, responsible, compassionate, well-spoken, sensible and refined, loyal and steadfast in love, never abandoning.
