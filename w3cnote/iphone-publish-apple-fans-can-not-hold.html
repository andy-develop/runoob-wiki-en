iPhone is about to be released, Apple fans can't hold back

Categories Internet

Now iPhones can also make your face look smaller. Samsung, are you scared?

Lonely, sad, alone, tired? Every night at 10, Code Buddy keeps you company in bed!

This column is strongly sponsored by the Programmer Singles Association, "I certainly won't be lonely all my life."

After a brief appearance on Tuesday, many people said the name "Low Buddy" was hard to remember. I went home and had a fortune-teller count on his fingers; sure enough, it was true. So I immediately changed my name. From now on, just call me by my Chinese name "Code Buddy". I feel so cute~

It's Friday again. Looking back at the tech news from this week, I'm afraid the iPhone launch event on September 9 is what our friends are most looking forward to. Speaking of which, Code Buddy is also a little excited~

The wallet that has been saving up for a whole year can't wait anymore. It urgently needs new "ingredients" — let the iPhone 6 come even more fiercely!

Think about all those countless days in the past when I ate instant noodles to save money for an iPhone, and now my wish is finally about to come true! Just thinking about it is so exciting. Ahaha, cough, cough, cough... Sorry, I was too excited and choked on my laughter.

The feelings of a loser are not something you'd understand. Looking back on those days of saving money, I also advised myself not to work so hard, and on special occasions I could add an extra meal. Beef, pork, or seafood? I once fell into such contemplation.

In the end, I decided to eat all three packs together, and add an egg! I immediately felt high-end and classy. (Smug~)

Thinking that the iPhone 6 is coming out in another month, my heart is like an arrow flying home!

Huh, did I use that idiom wrong?

Speaking of which, if I end up buying a "pear" phone, that would be such a tragedy.

It is reported that this time Applewill release two iPhone models, one with a 4.7-inch screen and one with a 5.5-inch screen.

Now iPhones can also make your face look smaller. Samsung, are you scared?

Samsung says: Heh, heh, heh.

According to the latest data released by market research firm Strategy Analytics on Wednesday, thanks to the strong sales of its flagship smartphone Galaxy S5 in the North American market, Samsung's North American smartphone market share reached 36.2% in the second quarter, surpassing Apple for the first time to become the market leader.

Is this a sign that they're going to strike right at your doorstep?

Apple: I haven't been the big brother for many years. (via Golden Crispy Chicken)

The two new iPhones released on September 9 are said to adopt a brand-new appearance, and the specs for the chip and camera will also be improved.

Code Buddy sincerely hopes Apple will also improve the iPhone's battery. Recharging once a day is truly unbearable.

However, there is a new technology that is said to solve this problem. Recently, a student from Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology invented a "body-heat power bank" that can use temperature differences to convert body heat into electrical energy, thereby charging a phone.

It is said that the body-heat power bank has a conversion rate of 17%. Holding it in your hand for 2 hours can fully charge an iPhone.

Sounds really good. But the question is, how can you maintain a 20°C temperature difference during the charging process? You can't exactly go learn the "Ice and Fire Palm" just to charge an iPhone, can you?

In any case, I still hope to see the product go on the market soon. After all, any idea that hopes to improve the current situation is worth encouraging. No more words—go for it, young man!

Apple is about to release a new phone; fruit fans are all smiles.

They started saving money a year ago; now they're just skin and bones.

Skin and bones is no big deal; their hearts are set only on the new phone.

Only when the phone is in hand will life have meaning.
