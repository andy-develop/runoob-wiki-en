Category Programmer Jokes

1.

A programmer asked Kobe: "Why are you so successful?"

Kobe: "Do you know what Los Angeles looks like at 4 a.m.?"

Programmer: "I do. That's usually when I'm still writing code. What's up?"

Kobe: "Uh..."

2.

Goddess: "If you can get everyone on this forum to fight, I'll go with you tonight."

Programmer: "PHP is the best language!"

The forum blew up with all kinds of arguments.

Goddess: "I'm impressed. Let's go, you can do whatever you want."

Programmer: "Not today. I have to convince them that PHP is the best language."

3.

I'm a miserable programmer. Tonight I worked overtime almost until dawn, so sleepy I could barely keep my eyes open. My female boss was very concerned and asked if I wanted a midnight snack. I said grumpily, "Forget the midnight snack, just let me get some sleep." She blushed, said coyly, "How annoying!" then sat down next to me without moving, staying awfully close, which made me nervous. Could she have discovered a bug in my program?

4.

You must marry a programmer!!!!!!

Lots of money, few words, dies early

5.

A wife called her programmer husband: "Buy a pound of buns on your way home from work. If you see a watermelon seller, buy one."

That night, the programmer husband came home holding a single bun...

His wife angrily said: "Why did you only buy one bun?!"

The husband replied: "Because I saw the watermelon seller."

Someone posted: "Everyone, I want to become a programmer. What should I pay attention to..."

A programmer: "Wait until I get off work and I'll tell you in detail..."

And then... there was no "and then."

8.

I asked my programmer friend to lend me 1,000. He said, "Let me lend you 24 more, to round it up."

9.

Programmer A: "I'll have the fish-flavored shredded pork rice bowl. What about you?"

Programmer B: "Kung Pao chicken rice bowl."

Programmer A wrote on the order form:

Fish-flavored shredded pork rice bowl 1

Kung Pao chicken rice bowl 1

Programmer B: "I'd rather have the beef noodles!"

Programmer A corrected the order form:

Fish-flavored shredded pork rice bowl 1

// Kung Pao chicken rice bowl 1

Beef noodles 1

10.

I'm a programmer. One day I sat by the roadside drinking water and desperately hunting for a bug, when a beggar sat down next to me and started begging. I felt sorry for him, so I gave him a dollar and went back to debugging. Business was probably slow, so he idly watched what I was doing. After a while, he said softly: "You're missing a semicolon here."

I asked in surprise: "You know this field too?" The beggar said: "I used to do this."

11.

After retiring, a programmer decided to practice calligraphy, so he spent a fortune on the Four Treasures of the Study. One day after a meal, he was suddenly in an elegant mood. He ground the ink, laid out the paper, and lit fine sandalwood incense. After calming himself a moment, he splashed ink and wrote a line with solemnity: hello world!

12.

"IT work is too tiring. What should I do if I want to switch to another line?" "Press the Enter key."

13.

Programmers don't like Qianlong's eighth son, because "八阿哥" (Ba Age, meaning "Eighth Prince") sounds like "bug."

14.

A female student was eating in the cafeteria when a programmer sidled up to her: "Excuse me, can I talk to you? I haven't talked to a girl in a month."

15.

Two programmers were chatting: "I met a hot girl yesterday. I took her home, and we immediately started kissing like crazy. She sat on my keyboard, and then..." "You have a computer at home too? What CPU model is it?"
