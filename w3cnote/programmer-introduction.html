Category Programmer Jokes

  • [Properties] This product varies in shape; the waist area (middle part) may be slightly enlarged.
  • [Functions & Indications] Primarily treats "client requirement change compulsion." Especially effective for clueless clients and idiot bosses, though the latter may have some side effects and complications.
  • [Usage & Dosage] Take freely, no dietary restrictions needed. Effect is more noticeable when taken during overtime work.
  • [Precautions] Use alone whenever possible; do not share with friends, otherwise it may likely be stolen by them. If used for an extended period, carefully observe for any adverse reactions. If the dosage is too high, this product may suddenly deteriorate or become invalid.
  • [Specifications] Specifications vary; no need to worry.
  • [Storage] Prefers indoor storage. If there are electronic devices such as computers or phones at home, place this product in front of these devices. Note: this product is not afraid of radiation.   • [Packaging] Usually somewhat crude. Note: rough appearance does not affect efficacy.
  • [Shelf Life] This product can be stored long-term, but performance may change over time. If stored for more than 40 years, it may become completely ineffective. Note: imported versions of this product last longer; domestic production currently cannot achieve this.

  • [Manufacturer] This product is produced in various settings, mainly including cultivation by professional manufacturers, natural self-generation, small workshop production, and mutation from other items. Note: after repeated clinical trials, there is no correlation between the legitimacy of the production site and the performance of the product itself.

[Special Notes]
