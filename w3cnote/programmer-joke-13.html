10 sentences that instantly enrage programmers, devastatingly powerful ~

Categories Programmer Jokes

10th place: "Keep it up! I'm leaving work now~"

As the requester, I remind you that you shouldn't say such things...

9th place: "You know what? My new computer has 1 TB of memory!"

Do you know the difference between memory and hard drive?

8th place: "Can you hack XXX's QQ for me?"

No! No! No!

7th place: "Finish it as soon as possible, okay?"

Saying this in an email makes it even more devastating...

6th place: "Just make a rough one first, let me take a look for reference."

Excuse me, what! does! rough! mean?!

5th place: "Help me add 10 features first, I need them tomorrow, please!"

Come on, I'm not a magician!

4th place: "Great, you haven't gone home yet. Help me with something, it'll be quick!"

Say this together with the first sentence, and you'll die very quickly~

3rd place: "Why is the internet so slow today?"

So it's my fault?

2nd place: "This should be pretty simple, right?"

I don't want to explain it to you; even if I do, you won't understand. I'm mentally exhausted.

1st place: "Just change it here and it's done!"

YOU CAN YOU UP ！
