The Secret Manual of Programmer Jokes

Getting Started

In college, I had just learned to use the internet, so I searched online for porn, and found a file called PHPbig全.rmvb. I thought it was a collection of porn videos, but it turned out to be a PHP video tutorial, so that's how I learned PHP...\(╯-╰)/

A programmer decided to practice calligraphy after retirement, so he spent a fortune on the Four Treasures of the Study. One day, after a meal, he suddenly felt inspired. He ground ink, laid out paper, and lit fine sandalwood incense. After calming his mind for a moment, he splashed ink and boldly wrote a line: hello world!

Single Life

A programmer asked the Zen master: "Master, I'm healthy and have a proper mindset, and I'm good in every way, so why don't I have a girlfriend?" The master smiled and replied: "The reason is simple, but if you want to know, you need to write a piece of Java code first." The young man paused for a moment, then finished writing. "Write a piece of C#." Finished. "Write a piece of PHP." Finished. "This is the reason."

"Hurry up and eat, after eating go back and continue writing code." "What's the use of writing code? Still no girlfriend, humph!" "What's the use of a girlfriend? She can't help you write code!"

"When you use pirated software, do you ever think about the programmers who made this software?! How are they supposed to support their families?"

"Hahaha, stop joking. Programmers don't have families to support!"

I'm a miserable programmer. Tonight I worked overtime almost all night, and I was so sleepy I could barely keep my eyes open. My female boss was very concerned and asked if I wanted a midnight snack. I said grumpily, "Forget the snack, just let me get some sleep." She blushed and said "How annoying", then sat down next to me without moving, seeming very close, which made me nervous. Could she have discovered a bug in my program?

Expert Chapter

  • A: Hey, what does // mean?
  • B: Hey.
  • A: Um, I'm asking what // means?
  • B: Go ahead and ask.
  • A: Didn't I just ask?
  • B: Huh?
  • A: Look at the chat log again...
  • B: Done reading.
  • A: ......So what is //?
  • B: So what?
  • A: You're deliberately messing with me, right?
  • B: No, what do you want to ask?

After this endless loop, A angrily broke off the friendship with B and studied programming hard by himself.

Years later, A finally achieved success. Thinking of B, he looked at the chat log again, and suddenly realized that B hadn't been messing with him...

And he himself no longer knew what question he had actually asked B back then...

PS: // In programming, it's a comment...

Future Trailer

I'm a programmer. One day I sat by the roadside drinking water while painstakingly checking my program. A beggar sat down next to me and started begging. I felt sorry for him and gave him 1 yuan. Then I continued debugging. His business was probably bad, so he idly watched what I was doing. After a while, he slowly pointed at my screen and said, "You're missing a semicolon here."
