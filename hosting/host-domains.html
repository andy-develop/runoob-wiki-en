What is a domain name?

A domain name is the unique name of a website, for exampleexample.com。

Domain names need to be registered. Once a domain name is registered, it is added to a large domain registrar, along with information related to your website - including IP information stored on DNS servers.

DNS refers to the Domain Name System. DNS servers are responsible for informing other computers on the internet about your domain name and address information.

Register domain

You can register a domain name through a domain registration company.

These companies all provide interfaces for checking available domain names and offer domain suffixes that can be registered at the same time.

Select domain

For any individual or organization, choosing a domain name is a very important step.

While domain names are being registered in large numbers, new domain suffixes and creative ideas still provide thousands of great options.

When choosing a domain name, it is important to consider its purpose, as this will make it easier for people to find your website.

Excellent domain names have the following characteristics:

Brief- People don't like typing! The shorter the domain name, the easier it is to access, and the less likely users are to make typing errors.

meaningful- A short domain name without meaning is not necessarily good. 34i4nh69.com is only 8 characters long, but it is neither easy to type nor easy to remember. So please choose a domain name that is relevant to your website and easy to understand.

Clear- Clarity is also important when choosing a domain name. Avoid names that are difficult to spell or pronounce. Also pay attention to whether the domain name sounds pleasing to the ear and can be quickly communicated over the phone.

exposure rate- Just as high-end real estate projects can achieve extremely high exposure, a short and memorable domain name is also an asset. In addition to visitors using your domain name, you should also consider search engines. Search engines index and rate your website based on the relevance of people's online search queries. To maximize your website's exposure, you can include relevant search terms in your domain name. Of course, the premise is still that the domain name you choose should be short, clear, and meaningful.

subdomain

Most people do not realize it, but they use subdomains every day. The most common "www" is actually a typical subdomain. Subdomains can be created on DNS servers and do not need to be registered through a domain registrar. Of course, before creating a subdomain, you still need to register the original domain name first. Many examples of subdomains can be seen on the internet: for example, c.example.com and test.example.com.

You may ask your website hosting provider to create subdomains, or you can do so by managing your DNS server.

Fake Domains - Directory Listing

Some providers may offer you a unique name under their own domain: www.theircompany.com/yourcompany/

This is not a real domain name, but a directory - this kind of situation should be avoided as much as possible.

Such a URL is not worth having, especially for a company. So avoid using it if you can afford to pay for a domain name. This type of URL is typically used by ISPs for personal or free websites, essentially a way to share an independent domain and provide users with their own address.

Full competition among domain registrars has greatly reduced domain registration fees, so this way of sharing domain names is becoming rarer, because people only need $15 to register their own domain name.

After you have chosen - and registered - your own domain name, be sure to use it on all your web pages, and on all correspondence, such as email and traditional letters.

Making your domain name known to others is important—tell your partners and customers about it.
